# Rust-only features

What the Rust engine has that game.js, the frozen fallback, does not, and where each lives.

- **The transformation scene** (`planet-core/src/xform.rs`, `planet-render/src/xform.rs`). A form-up is a scene, not one frame: for 7 s (3.5 s with reduced motion) he powers up on top of the planet, which turns to face us. The sky darkens behind it, one giant アア! shout sits behind the planet, cropped by the card and shaking harder as the power builds. Rocks tear loose and rise, lightning strikes, and his aura, which doubles as the tap meter, swells and kicks out with every tap. A column of light punches up out of him. Then the form lands: the flash, a sunburst in the form's colours for 3 s (1.5 s), the rocks flung away, and the form's name in the usual toast. While he powers up every tap goes into it (+2% each); 6 or more earn a power surge for 20 s, +1 in the timed buffs' sum (×2 on its own, as the boost's is), named in the same toast and in the buffs line. Idle, it lands on its own. On snake way, and for a form a preview hook or a test's poke of `S` sets up, it lands at once, as before. `#xform` opens on the scene. The widget (`bare`) keeps its transparency: no sky or sunburst, while the shout, the lightning, the rocks, the aura and the column are drawn over the desktop. There the two characters aren't cropped: they are sized and placed to fit whole inside the frame at any widget size, side by side behind the planet, still shaking. `planet-core/tests/xform.rs` checks the AFK rule over the 45 s from each of the first three form-ups (one shared history, then each way of playing it, over 24 seeds). Idle, the scene earns ×0.89 of an instant form: he poses for 7 s instead of running laps. An active player earns ×1.07 of an instant form, and ×1.30 over leaving the scene alone. Active play earns ×2.81 of idle (×2.34 with instant forms). (From the first form-up alone, with snake way still in the rotation, these were ×0.90, ×0.96, ×1.16, ×2.64 and ×2.47; parking it made that window the first super saiyan's, ×3.58.) The surge was tuned on the first-form-up numbers: ×1.5 for 30 s left an active player ×0.92 of an instant form.
- **The shop's bulk buys and hover card** (`planet-core/src/buy.rs`, page.html). Shift-click buys ×10 and alt-click max; touch has a ×1 / ×10 / max toggle above the list, and the buttons show that many's price. ×10 is all or nothing and is the ten single buys in a row; max is single buys until you can't, counting the levels too cheap to change what you hold in one search, and never spends past what you hold. Hovering a row (a long press on touch) shows what its next level does, its effect on your rate, and the prices of the next level, ten and max, all from planet-core (`Game::shop_info_json`). None of it changes income; `planet-core/tests/buy.rs` checks ×10 against ten single buys, max against single buys until you can't, and the card's numbers. Nothing in the golden scenarios uses them, and none of it is in the state samples, so the traces don't move.
- **A saga boss's arrival** (`planet-core/src/intro.rs`, `planet-render/src/intro.rs`). Vegeta's scouter eyepiece (green glass, a dark frame, the white housing and its red lamp) swings in small. Its reading climbs in small LCD digits, the only text, inside the glass, while the glass swells to fill the view, jitters, fills with static and cracks; it reads OVERLOAD and ERR, shatters across the whole view, and he stands revealed, locked in red brackets. The toast: "the scouter can't take it! <boss>, power N" (his health). It plays by itself in 4.1 s; the fight's 40 s, his ki blasts and the beam struggle wait for it. Any tap, beam or big bang skips to the shatter and starts the fight. Reduced motion: no flash, shake, jitter, static or blinking, and the glass falls away slowly. On the bare widget the glass is fainter, so the desktop shows through. `#sagaN` opens on it.
- **Boss mechanics, wave 1** (`planet-core/src/mech.rs`, `planet-render/src/mech.rs`), for saga raids (and universe 11's Jiren). They start 2.5 s or more into the fight, pause during a beam struggle, and run on fixed cadences (no random draws but particle bursts). Their penalties land on active inputs only: taps on him, beams and big bangs. Idle ki and passive damage are never touched, so idle pays only Cell's small heal and Buu's one reform. Words go in toasts; the canvas has shapes and colour, plus one small "+4". **Frieza**: every ~5 s he aims a pink death-beam laser at your fighter (a pulsing ring and a draining timer bar on him). A tap on your fighter deflects it back for 3% of his hp; otherwise you're stunned for 1.5 s (no taps on him, beams or big bangs) and lose your combo. **Cell**: below 90% hp his segmented tail grabs an ally (bubbles, piccolo, krillin in turn), and yellow ki runs up it as he heals 4%/s for 3 s, at most 5% a fight. His hp bar refills with a small "+4". A tap on the ally rescues him for a 5% punch. **Kid Buu**: at 25% he glows pink (outline, steam, a pink bar notched at 25%). A small hit that would finish him makes him reform once, at 4%. A beam charged past 60%, a big bang, the spirit bomb or a won struggle finishes him outright. **Hit**: a glint, then a 1 s time skip: the world turns violet under a sweeping clock face, he steps closer and leaves an afterimage, and your taps, beams and big bangs do nothing. Then a 0.6 s opening where taps on him land ×2. **Jiren**: a red aura. A beam under full charge bounces back red and stuns you for 1.2 s; a full one breaks through for ×1.5. A gauge under the hero shows the charge against the full mark, and the aim line turns gold at full. Cell, the one boss taller than 40 px, stands low enough that his name and bar stay on the canvas. `planet-core/tests/bosses.rs` checks the AFK rule over 12 seeds; its numbers are in the pull request.
- **Snake way is parked** until a better idea comes: the trip rotation skips its slot (`SNAKE_WAY_ENABLED` in `planet-core/src/data.rs`, false), so the 3rd trip goes to beerus' planet and a rotation is 10 trips. Its code stays (the run in `timeline.rs`, the drawing in `planet-render/src/snake.rs`) and `#snake` still runs it. Set the const to true to put it back. `planet-core/tests/trips.rs` checks a long run never goes there and that a rotation's income holds: per second of base income, parked earns ×1.02 of before idle and ×1.03 active (16 seeds; ×1.013 and ×1.020 ± 0.02 over 40), so nothing of its pay was moved elsewhere. Its four achievements (`SNAKE_ACH`: `snakeway`, `hfil`, `streak`, `souls10`) are hidden from the menu and the share card while it's parked, so the count is out of 77; they come back with it.

- **Ki limits and number milestones** (`planet-core/src/limits.rs`, `planet-render/src/limits.rs`). The ki ceiling stops being a quiet limit:
  - **Strain drives the creep.** Once the multiverse opens it creeps ×1.04 a minute of **strain**, not of play time. Idle play is 1 strain a second, so idle pace is unchanged. Training taps (one every 0.3s counts, +0.45), full-charge beams (+10), big bangs (+6) and kaioken (×1.4) add more.
  - **Limits.** The creep stops at the current limit: limit I at a ceiling of 1e6, each later one at twice the exponent (1e12, 1e24 …). Pinned there with the engine past it, strain fills the break meter instead: 20 minutes for limit I, ×1.2 for each one after.
  - **A break.** Time stops: the world drains to violet-grey, the grand priest's halo settles over the planet and the frozen world cracks round him, then the colour floods back out from him. Then:
    - the limit becomes a floor under the engine (it multiplies `core()`, and keeps it at least there right after god ki);
    - the lid lifts to the next limit, and the creep doubles;
    - limits I–V bring named rewards: the grand priest takes notice, the seed soft cap's exponent goes 0.5 → 0.75, every later break is a form-up, god ki compounds (×1.5ⁿ when that's more than 1+n), and universe stars become ×1.75;
    - from limit III each break adds a **limit-breaker** form (×2 each, the last canon form's look, its own aura), landed by the transformation scene once the break's scene is over.
  - **Nothing erases a limit**: ascending, god ki and zeno keep the limits, the creep and the floor.
  - **Milestones.** These go by lifetime best: 1e10 (the scouter wave, every scouter in the sky pops), 1e100 (the sky splits on the gods' realm), 1.8e308 (it splits red: beerus), 1e1000 and 1e10,000 (claps of thunder), 1e100,000 (four claps), 1e1,000,000 (the scouter wave in gold), then every ×10 of the exponent, in turn. Small beats come between them on each step of the leading power of ten (1e20 … 1e90, 1e200, 1e300 …): a burst and the toast.
    - Each gives ×2 for 30s. The text is the usual toast; the standing line reads "ki limit III (1.0Sp) — strain 63% · next: 1.0e40".
  - Hooks: `#limitN` (limit N about to break), `#mileK` (the K-th named milestone landing).
  - `planet-core/tests/limits.rs` checks the ladder and that a limit breaks idle. It also checks that the floor holds through god ki and zeno, and that the forms come from limit III. Its ignored `pace` test plays idle and active bots from a new game (see the PR for the table).

- **Looks the milestones unlock** (`planet-core/src/looks.rs`). The named milestones at 1e10, 1e100 and 1e1000 each unlock one look of every kind, and a toast names them:
  - an aura colour (scouter green, the gods' violet, zeno's gold);
  - something stood on the planet (the broken scouter on a pedestal, whis's staff, zeno's button);
  - a share-card frame (the scouter's readout, the angels' ring, zeno's stars);
  - a trail behind the hero as he runs (ki sparks, starlight, confetti).
  
  Like everything else they last the run: nothing is saved but the daily bonus's date. The menu's achievements tab wears them, one of each kind (`wear`, in [HUD.md](HUD.md)). Hook: `#looks0` (`#looks1`, `#looks3`) wears that milestone's set.

- **The ramen break** (`planet-core/src/ramen.rs`, `planet-render/src/snack.rs`). Now and then (after 150 s of quiet, then every 160–220 s of it: fights, scenes, trips, the great ape, the nimbus and the time chamber hold the clock) the hero stops mid-lap for a bowl of ramen, and the planet turns him gently our way. He stands with the bowl at his chest, steam rising, a noodle riding the chopsticks up. Left alone for 8 s he runs on with a full belly, ×1.5 for 30 s (in the buffs line). A tap on him knocks the bowl over: the broth spills, no full belly, and he sulks, tired ×0.75 for 5 s. Training taps (anywhere but on him) go on as usual. Anything big that starts meanwhile has him gulp it down, full belly and all. No random draws. `planet-core/tests/ramen.rs` checks the AFK rule over 12 seeds: from a break, idle earns ×1.16 of no break over 58 s (×1.07 over 2 minutes); training while leaving him be earns ×3.28 (×2.47) of idle, and knocking the bowl over ×0.87 of leaving it. Hook: `#ramen`.
- **Bubbles chases things** (`planet-core/src/bubbles.rs`). Bubbles stops trotting behind the hero: in turn he heels, wanders off ahead or behind and sniffs about, and runs to the nearest ki orb or dragon ball lying ahead. He faces where he's going. A tap on him makes him dance (hopping and turning about); the tap still trains. It changes only where he stands: his income doesn't, and Cell's grab and the fusion offer find him where he is. Reduced motion: no hops, slower turns. No random draws. Hook: `#bubbles`.
- **The Cell Games** (`planet-core/src/cellgames.rs`, `planet-render/src/games.rs`). A story beat 20 minutes of play after Cell's saga is won, then a repeat every 45–55 minutes of play; each waits for a quiet moment at home and is sized on the rate, like everything else. Cell lays his tiled ring on the planet (it turns the ring our way) and waits in it over ten countdown pips. Then five Cell Jr.s hop round it: a tap gets one (4 s of rate), and Gohan shoots one every 3 s on his own. Then perfect Cell steps up as an ordinary boss (not a saga boss: no scouter intro and no wave-1 mechanic), and taps, beams, big bangs and the idle ki blasts wear him down. Beaten, he swells up to self-destruct, and your beam and his blast meet between you. Gohan pushes on his own, and taps, beams (×charge) and big bangs push harder. A tap into one of its flares (every 2 s) pushes back. Beamed back: 30 s of rate and champion ×2 for 30 s. If it goes off, King Kai's planet goes up with him: no prize, and halos on everyone for 2 minutes, nothing else. Idle, Gohan wins every phase, slowly. While the games run no saga boss, universe fight, tournament, movie, storm or trip starts, and `calm()` is false (no instant transmission). Reduced motion: no hops, flicker or wobble; the flares show as a steady wash. Hook: `#cellgames`.
- **Majin Buu's candy** (`planet-core/src/candy.rs`, `planet-render/src/games.rs`). A story beat 90 s of play after Kid Buu's saga is won, then a repeat every 45–55 minutes of play, at a quiet moment at home. Fat Buu (a new sprite) comes as an ordinary boss for 40 s. Every 4 s his antenna zaps the next thing on his list: on King Kai's planet a tree (gumdrop-pink leaves, candy-cane trunk), a capsule house (pink and white swirls), bubbles or piccolo (a wrapped sweet); elsewhere a lollipop on the floor. Candy produces nothing: bubbles' and piccolo's income stop, and the houses lose a candied house's share. A tap on candy turns it back, with a sugar rush (×1.5 for 15 s, 2 s of rate). Beat him (the usual fight) and everything turns back, with his boss prize. Wait him out and he floats off bored; the candy turns back and melts into a sugar high (×1.5 for 30 s). Hook: `#candy`.
- `planet-core/tests/events.rs` checks the AFK rule for both over 12 seeds. Idle with the event, against `no_events`: the Cell Games ×1.51 over 120 s, the candy ×0.97 over 90 s, and each resolves on its own. Active against idle: ×2.20 (×2.59 in the same window without the games, whose taps go to the event instead) and ×4.48 (×3.66 without). It also checks that the games come as the story beat, then again 45 minutes or more later.
